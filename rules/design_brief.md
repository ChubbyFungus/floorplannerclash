# Kitchen Design Brief

This document outlines the information needed to create a 3D floor plan for a kitchen.

## Room Details

- What are the dimensions of the room (width and depth in feet)?
- What is the ceiling height?

## Style

- What is the desired style for the kitchen? (e.g., Modern, Traditional, Farmhouse)
- What are the preferred colors? (e.g., primary color, accent color)

## Appliances

- What appliances do you need? (e.g., refrigerator, oven, dishwasher, microwave)
- Do you have any specific requirements for the appliances? (e.g., size, type, finish)

## Layout

- What is the desired layout for the kitchen? (e.g., L-shaped, U-shaped, Galley)
- Where are the doors and windows located?

## Cabinets

- What style of cabinets do you prefer? (e.g., Shaker, Flat-panel)
- What color and finish do you want for the cabinets?

## Countertops

- What material do you want for the countertops? (e.g., Quartz, Granite, Marble)
- What color and finish do you want for the countertops?

## Flooring

- What type of flooring do you want? (e.g., Hardwood, Tile, Vinyl)
